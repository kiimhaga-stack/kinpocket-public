Friend Tracker world map (worldmap.js, worldmap-data.js)

Map data: Natural Earth, 1:50m Cultural Vectors, "Admin 0 - Countries" (ne_50m_admin_0_countries),
from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector (naturalearthdata.com).
License: public domain. "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data found on this website
are in the public domain. You may use the maps in any manner, including modifying the content and design,
electronic dissemination, and offset printing." (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)
Credit (optional, given anyway): Made with Natural Earth.

Processing for Friend Tracker: pre-projected to the Natural Earth I projection, simplified with
Douglas-Peucker (tolerance 0.18 map units), coordinates rounded to 0.1 unit, tiny islands dropped
(every country keeps at least its largest polygon). Country borders follow Natural Earth's
de facto boundaries; they are not a statement on any territorial dispute.

worldmap.js (renderer) is part of Friend Tracker.

State/province borders (worldmap-adm1.js: United States, Mexico, Canada, Russia, China, India, Brazil, Australia): Natural Earth 1:10m Cultural Vectors,
Admin 1 – States, Provinces (boundary lines), public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/. Pre-projected and simplified for Friend Tracker (2026-10-21).
